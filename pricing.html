Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data uploaded or stored?

No. The DocTable statement engine operates client-side or ephemerally. Transactions are parsed in memory, exported directly to your computer as XLSX/CSV, and never stored in any persistent database.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed monthly and can be cancelled with a single click inside your billing portal. You will retain access until the end of your billing period.

Which payment methods are accepted?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized bank transfer / PromptPay via Stripe and Lemon Squeezy Merchant of Record.

Can I self-host this on my own infrastructure?

Yes! The entire suite is built on zero-dependency native Node.js and static HTML/Tailwind. You can deploy it to Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, or run it locally in Docker with zero bloat.
